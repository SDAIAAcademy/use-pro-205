Sawaed launches Jiwar in 12 cities on 10 January 2027

1 The Sawaed National Volunteering Authority will launch Jiwar in 12 cities on 10 January 2027, after a six-month pilot in Riyadh, Jeddah and Abha. The service matches volunteers with requests from people aged 60 or more and people with disabilities in their own neighbourhoods.

2 Between 1 June and 30 November 2026 the pilot received 4,860 requests, of which 4,221 were completed (86.7%). It registered 3,150 volunteers, 1,940 of them active, who served 2,305 beneficiaries. The average time from a request to its assignment to a volunteer was 26 hours.

3 Dr. Muthana Al-Helwa, the Authority's Chief Executive, said: "Jiwar turns helping a neighbour into an organised service: a clear request, a verified volunteer, and follow-up until the request is done."

4 The service offers three kinds of help: daily errands, accompaniment to appointments and simple home maintenance. Of the 1,120 beneficiaries who answered the survey, 91% said they were satisfied with the service.

5 Baraa Al-Mushawwah, a volunteer from Abha, said: "I give two hours a week, and before I leave home I know whom I will help and with what."

6 The Authority aims to register 15,000 volunteers by the end of 2027. Registration is open to anyone aged 18 or more through the Jiwar app and jiwar.sawaed.example.
